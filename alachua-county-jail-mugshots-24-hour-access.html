This legal framework often mandates the disclosure of booking photographs upon request, and increasingly, via online platforms. The shift from physical requests at a sheriff's office to instantaneous online access marks a significant evolution in how these records are consumed by the public.

Unfolding Implications of Continuous Digital Presence
