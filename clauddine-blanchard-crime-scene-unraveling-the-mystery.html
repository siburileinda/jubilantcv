The Facade of Frailty

Gypsy Rose was presented as suffering from a litany of severe ailments, including leukemia, muscular dystrophy, vision and hearing impairments, and even requiring a feeding tube and wheelchair. Communities rallied around them, offering donations, trips, and even a custom-built house.
