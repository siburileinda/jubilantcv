The proliferation of third-party websites that aggregate arrest data and mugshots has also significantly altered the landscape. While these platforms often claim to provide a public service by centralizing information, they frequently operate without the same regulatory oversight as official government sites, raising concerns about accuracy, context, and the potential for these records to permanently follow individuals, regardless of conviction or exoneration.
