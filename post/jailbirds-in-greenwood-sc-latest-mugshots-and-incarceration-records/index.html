Understanding Mugshots and Incarceration Records#

Mugshots are photographs taken of individuals after they have been arrested, typically as part of the booking process. These images, along with other details such as the individual’s name, charges, and sometimes the bail amount, are compiled into records known as incarceration records or jail records. These records serve as a public document of an individual’s arrest and detention history, providing transparency and accountability within the law enforcement system. In Greenwood, SC, like elsewhere, these records are often made available to the public, either through official government websites, third-party databases, or local law enforcement agencies. The Criminal Justice Process in Greenwood, SC#

Understanding the criminal justice process in Greenwood, SC, is essential for putting the context of jailbirds and their records into perspective. When an individual is arrested, they are typically taken into custody by law enforcement officers. The next step involves booking, where the individual’s personal information, fingerprints, and a mugshot are taken. After booking, the individual may be released on bail or kept in custody pending a court hearing. The court process involves arraignment, where charges are formally presented, followed by potential pre-trial hearings, a trial, and finally, sentencing if found guilty.
