What is a Discord User ID#

A Discord user ID is a unique string of numbers that identifies a user on the platform. It’s like a fingerprint, distinguishing one user from another. This ID is used by Discord to keep track of user accounts, permissions, and other relevant information. While your username might be visible to others, your user ID remains hidden, hence the term “secret ID.” Knowing your user ID can come in handy in various situations, such as when you need to provide it to a developer for a specific bot or application, or when troubleshooting issues with your account. A Step-by-Step Guide to Finding Your Discord User ID#

Finding your Discord user ID is relatively straightforward, but it does require a few specific steps. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you get your Discord username’s secret ID:

  1. Enable Developer Mode: The first step to finding your user ID is to enable Developer Mode on Discord. To do this, open Discord and navigate to the “Settings” icon, represented by a gear, usually found in the bottom left corner of the screen. Click on “Settings,” then select “Appearance” from the left-hand menu. Scroll down to the “Advanced” section and toggle the switch next to “Developer Mode” to enable it.

  2. Go to Your User Settings: After enabling Developer Mode, go back to the main Discord window. You should now see a “Copy ID” option when you right-click on a user, including yourself. To find your own user ID, right-click on your username in the member list or in a chat where you are mentioned.

  3. Right-Click and Copy ID: Once you’ve located your username, right-click on it. A context menu will appear with several options. Click on “Copy ID” to copy your user ID to the clipboard. You can then paste this ID into any application or document that requires it.

  • Troubleshooting: If you’re experiencing issues with your Discord account, having your user ID ready can speed up the troubleshooting process with Discord’s support team.

Frequently Asked Questions#

What is the difference between my Discord username and user ID?#

Your Discord username is the name that appears in chats and member lists, while your user ID is a unique string of numbers that Discord uses to identify your account internally.

Can I change my Discord user ID?#

No, your user ID is assigned by Discord when you create your account and cannot be changed. It is unique to your account and is used by Discord to track your account activities and permissions.

Is it safe to share my user ID?#

It’s generally not recommended to share your user ID publicly or with untrusted sources, as it could potentially be used to access your account information or disrupt your Discord experience. However, sharing it with trusted developers or Discord support when necessary is usually safe.

How do I know if my user ID is correct?#

To verify your user ID, you can use online tools or bots that can look up user information based on the ID. Additionally, if you’re providing your ID for a specific service or bot, the developer or support team can confirm if the ID is correct and functional.
