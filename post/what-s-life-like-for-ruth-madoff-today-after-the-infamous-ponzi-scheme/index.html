The Ponzi Scheme Unfolds#

The Madoff Ponzi scheme, which started in the early 1990s, involved promising investors high returns on their investments. However, instead of investing the money, Madoff used it to pay off earlier investors and fund his own lavish lifestyle. The scheme relied on constant influxes of new investors to provide the returns for earlier investors. It was a house of cards waiting to collapse. In 2008, the scheme finally unraveled when Madoff was unable to meet the withdrawal demands of his investors. He was arrested in December 2008 and later sentenced to 150 years in prison.
