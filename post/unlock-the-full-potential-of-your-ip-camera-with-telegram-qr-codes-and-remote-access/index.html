Understanding IP Cameras and Their Capabilities

IP cameras, or internet protocol cameras, are digital cameras that can transmit video and audio signals over the internet. They are equipped with a range of features, including motion detection, night vision, and weather resistance, making them ideal for outdoor and indoor surveillance. IP cameras can be accessed remotely using a smartphone, tablet, or computer, allowing users to monitor their premises from anywhere in the world.
