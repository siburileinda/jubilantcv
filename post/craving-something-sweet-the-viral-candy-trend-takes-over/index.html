The Science Behind Our Love for Sweets#

But what is it about sweets that makes them so irresistible? The answer lies in the science behind our love for sweets. Our brains are wired to respond to sweet tastes, which is why we often crave sweets when we’re feeling stressed or emotional. The reason for this is that sweet tastes trigger the release of dopamine, a neurotransmitter that is associated with pleasure and reward. This is why sweets are often used as a comfort food, providing a temporary escape from the stresses of everyday life.
