Understanding Police Mugshots

Police mugshots are photographs taken of individuals who have been arrested or detained by law enforcement agencies. These photographs are typically taken at the time of booking, and they serve as a visual record of the arrest. Mugshots usually include a frontal view and a profile view of the individual, along with their name, date of birth, and other relevant information. The primary purpose of police mugshots is to help identify suspects, track criminal activity, and inform the public about recent arrests.
