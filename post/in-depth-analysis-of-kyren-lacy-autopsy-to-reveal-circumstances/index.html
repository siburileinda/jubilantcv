Understanding the Autopsy Process

Before diving into the specifics of the Kyren Lacy autopsy, it is essential to understand the process itself. The process involves a meticulous inspection of the body’s external and internal structures, including organs and tissues. The examination is typically conducted by a qualified forensic pathologist, who collects and analyzes specimens, such as blood and tissue samples, to aid in the investigation.
