The Rise and Fall of Pablo Escobar

Pablo Escobar’s rise to power began in the 1970s, when he started smuggling cocaine into the United States. His business acumen and ruthless tactics allowed him to quickly dominate the market, earning him the nickname “El Patrón” (The Boss). At the height of his power, Escobar was estimated to be worth over $30 billion, making him one of the richest men in the world. However, his success was short-lived, as the Colombian government, with the help of the United States, launched a crackdown on his empire.

The Hunt for Pablo Escobar

In 1991, Escobar surrendered to the Colombian authorities, but he soon escaped from prison and went into hiding. The hunt for Pablo Escobar was on, with the Colombian government and the United States offering large rewards for his capture. Manuela’s life became even more complicated, as she was forced to move from safe house to safe house, always looking over her shoulder.
