Early Life and Career

Born on September 26, 1968, in Mount Vernon, Washington, Jim Caviezel grew up in a tight-knit family with his parents, James and Maggie Caviezel, and his siblings. Caviezel’s interest in acting was piqued at a young age, and he pursued this passion by attending Mount Vernon High School, where he was an avid participant in the school’s drama club.
