The Turing Pharmaceuticals Controversy

Shkreli’s ascent to fame was not without its controversies, however. The company’s acquisition of the life-saving medication Daraprim, which was used to treat a rare parasitic infection, sparked outrage when Shkreli raised the price of the drug from $13.50 to $750 per pill. The move was widely condemned, with many accusing Shkreli of exploiting vulnerable patients for his own financial gain. The controversy surrounding Turing Pharmaceuticals and Daraprim would ultimately prove to be the catalyst for Shkreli’s downfall, as public outcry and government scrutiny turned the spotlight on the young pharmaceutical mogul.

The Fallout and Arrest

As the backlash against Turing Pharmaceuticals and Shkreli continued to grow, the young entrepreneur found himself at the center of a media firestorm. In 2015, Shkreli was arrested and charged with securities fraud related to his activities at Retrophin, marking the beginning of a long and tumultuous period in his life. Despite his claims of innocence, Shkreli was ultimately found guilty and sentenced to seven years in prison, bringing an abrupt end to his meteoric rise to fame.
