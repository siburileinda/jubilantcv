Accessing Public Records in Tarrant County, TX

Accessing public records in Tarrant County, TX can be done in person, online, or by mail. The County Clerk’s office and the District Clerk’s office provide online search tools and databases, making it possible for individuals to search for and access public records from the comfort of their own homes.

Online Search Tools

The Tarrant County Clerk’s office and the District Clerk’s office offer online search tools that allow users to search for public records by name, case number, or other criteria. These search tools are usually available on the agencies’ websites and can be accessed 24/7. Some of the online search tools available include:

  • The Tarrant County Clerk’s online records search, which allows users to search for public records, including property records, court records, and vital records.
  • The District Clerk’s online case search, which allows users to search for court records, including case files, judgments, and other related documents.

In-Person Access

Individuals can also access public records in person by visiting the County Clerk’s office, the District Clerk’s office, or the Tarrant County Sheriff’s Department. When visiting in person, individuals will need to provide identification and may need to fill out a request form to access the records they need.

Mugshots and Arrest Records

To access mugshots in Tarrant County, TX, individuals can visit the Tarrant County Sheriff’s Department website or the County Clerk’s office. The Tarrant County Sheriff’s Department provides an online inmate search tool that allows users to search for inmates by name or inmate number.
