Introduction to White Collar Crimes

White collar crimes, a term coined by Edwin Hardin Sutherland in 1939, refer to financially motivated, non-violent crimes committed by individuals or organizations for personal or professional gain. These crimes can range from embezzlement and fraud to insider trading and tax evasion. One notable case that highlights the complexities and severity of white collar crimes is that of John Bolz, a charismatic and cunning individual who committed a series of high-profile financial crimes. The Impact of White Collar Crime

The impact of white collar crime can be significant, affecting not only the individuals directly involved but also the broader economy and society.
