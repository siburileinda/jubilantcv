The Foundation of Transparency

The Labor Condition Application (LCA), a critical component of the U.S. H-1B visa program, serves as a public declaration by employers regarding their intent to hire foreign workers. It mandates that employers attest to specific conditions, including paying workers the prevailing wage for their occupation in the area of employment or the actual wage paid to other employees with similar experience and qualifications, whichever is higher. This regulatory requirement, enforced by the Department of Labor, generates a trove of data that, when analyzed, provides significant transparency into salary levels and job classifications within companies heavily reliant on visa-sponsored talent, such as TCS.

"LCA data offers an unparalleled window into the actual compensation practices of major H-1B employers. It's a goldmine for job seekers and market analysts alike, revealing granular details that traditional salary surveys often miss," remarked Dr. Arvin Sharma, a labor economist specializing in technology sector employment.
